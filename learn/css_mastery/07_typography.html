line-height — same three sentences, tight vs loose:

Tight line-height (1.1) crowds wrapped lines together, uncomfortable to read past a sentence or two — line-height 1.1 is really meant for a single-line heading, not a paragraph like this one.

Loose line-height (1.8) gives each line room to breathe, which is closer to what a body paragraph like this one actually wants for comfortable reading over several lines.
