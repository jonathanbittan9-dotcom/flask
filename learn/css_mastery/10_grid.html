Grid vs flexbox: how to choose

One dimension (a row of buttons, a navbar) → flexbox. Two dimensions at once (a whole page layout, a photo gallery, a dashboard) → grid. When in doubt: if you find yourself nesting flexboxes to fake rows AND columns together, that's usually grid's job instead.
